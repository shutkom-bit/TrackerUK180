Know how many days you can still spend abroad

For most routes to UK settlement you can't be away more than 180 days in any 12 months. Tracker UK180 counts every day for you, so one long trip doesn't cost you years.

In the UKAbroad

The rule is simple. Counting it isn't.

The Home Office looks at every possible 12-month period during your qualifying years. A trip in February can still count against you next January.

Only whole days abroad count. The day you leave and the day you come back are treated as days in the UK.

Questions

Which visas does it work for?

It is set up for the 180-days-in-any-12-months rule used by Skilled Worker and most 5-year routes, and has settings for 3-year (Global Talent, Innovator Founder) and 10-year (Long Residence) routes. Family-route rules can differ, so check the guidance for your exact route.

Is this legal or immigration advice?

No. It is a tool to keep an accurate record and do the counting. It counts every absence, including ones the Home Office may disregard, so it errs on the safe side. Before you apply, check the current Home Office guidance or speak to a regulated adviser.

Is this a substitute for a solicitor or adviser?

No. Tracker UK180 keeps an accurate record and does the arithmetic. It does not assess your eligibility or give legal advice. For questions about your own case, speak to a regulated immigration adviser or solicitor.

Where do the counting rules come from?

From the Home Office's Appendix Continuous Residence and the caseworker guidance on continuous residence, linked in the "Based on official guidance" section above. If the rules change, we update the tracker and you can download the new version.

Does it work on Mac, iPhone and Android?

Yes. The spreadsheet works in Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers. The interactive tracker opens in any modern browser on a computer or phone.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device: inside your spreadsheet file, or in your browser for the interactive version. Nothing is uploaded. The interactive version has a backup button so you can move your data to another device.
